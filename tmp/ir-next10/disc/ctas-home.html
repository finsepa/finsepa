Investor Relations

Cintas provides businesses with workwear and business essentials to meet image, safety, cleanliness and compliance needs.

The difference at Cintas is clear. Our people, products and services make us a trusted partner in delivering peace of mind. We help over one million businesses get Ready for the Workday® with a robust offering of products and service that our customers need to look and perform at their best every day.

Our investors are part of our success, propelling us toward deeper customer relationships, innovation in our product and service offering and an unsurpassed pledge to better the communities and customers we serve.

Market Opportunity:

  • Approximately 17 million businesses in the U.S. and Canada and we do business with a little over 1 million
  • Approximately 180 million people employed in the U.S. and Canada and we provide workwear solutions to about 5 million

Ways to Grow:

  • We sell new business to new customers, two-thirds of which comes from businesses that meet their needs by managing it themselves
  • We retain our customers by providing best-in-class products and services
  • We sell additional products and services to our existing customers
  • We pursue strategic M&A
