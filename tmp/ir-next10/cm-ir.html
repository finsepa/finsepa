Why invest in CIBC?

Our ambition is to be a client-focused, connected, and performance-driven bank, delivering industry-leading shareholder returns.

Strong leadership with a connected, purpose-led culture

Led by an experienced executive team and an engaged board committed to transparency and strong governance, our client-centric culture helps clients achieve their ambitions.

Diversified business mix and strong strategic execution to drive leading shareholder returns

Focused on delivering a premium ROE and strong earnings growth driven by high quality revenues and positive operating leverage.

A client-centric approach with distinct competitive advantages

We leverage our deep relationships and whole-bank capabilities to attract new clients, strengthen existing connections and capture strategic growth opportunities.

Disciplined risk management with robust financial strength

Our strong capital levels, disciplined allocation and strategic investments in technology, governance and controls support resilience, growth and enduring value.

Frequently asked questions 

Dividends on CIBC common shares are paid quarterly, subject to approval by CIBC's Board of Directors. For the latest dividend information, including amount, upcoming payment dates and dividend history, see our Common Dividends page. 

You can access current and past Annual Reports, Management Proxy Circulars and other disclosure documents on our Annual Reports page.

Information about our Annual General Meetings, including webcasts, voting results and related materials is available on our Annual Meetings page. 

On the SEDAR+ Opens a new window. website, for CIBC filings, enter “Canadian Imperial Bank of Commerce” in the search bar to view all relevant documents.

To access the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission’s EDGAR database of U.S. regulatory filings, access the SEC EDGAR Opens a new window. database and search for “Canadian Imperial Bank of Commerce.”

CIBC is audited by Ernst & Young LLP.

For a full list of equity analysts covering CIBC, please visit our Analyst Coverage page.

CIBC’s common shares are listed on the Toronto Stock Exchange (TSX) and the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE). You can buy or sell CIBC shares through a stockbroker registered with these exchanges. CIBC offers full-service brokerage through CIBC Wood Gundy Opens a new window. and direct investing services through CIBC Investor’s Edge Opens a new window. in Canada. 

CIBC's debt and deposit instruments are rated by DBRS, Moody's, Standard & Poor's and Fitch. You can find our latest credit ratings, outlooks and detailed rating information on our Credit Ratings page.

CIBC’s transfer agent is TSX Trust Company. They provide services related to shareholdings, dividend payments, lost certificates, duplicate mailings and the Shareholder Investment Plan. For detailed contact information and services, visit the Transfer Agent and Registrar section on our Contact Investor Relations page.

CIBC’s most recent stock split occurred on May 13, 2022, with a two-for-one split (by the way of a 100% stock dividend). For detailed information on stock splits and common share structure, visit our Common Share Description page. 

For information about CIBC’s preferred shares along with dividend history, rates and redemption dates, visit our Preferred Shares page.
