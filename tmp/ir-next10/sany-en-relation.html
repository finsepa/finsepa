Corporate governance structure

The company strictly complies with relevant laws and regulations, such as the Company Law of the People's Republic of China, the Securities Law of the People's Republic of China, the Guidelines for the Governance of Listed Companies, and the Listing Rules of the Shanghai Stock Exchange, as well as the Company's Articles of Association. The company continuously improves its corporate governance structure, establishes sound and standardized operating mechanisms, and actively enhances the depth and breadth of information disclosure. The company's shareholders' meeting, board of directors, and board of supervisors make scientific decisions in accordance with laws and regulations. Directors, supervisors, and senior management fulfill their duties faithfully and diligently, effectively safeguarding the company's interests and shareholders' legitimate rights and interests, and establishing a good image for the company in the capital market.
