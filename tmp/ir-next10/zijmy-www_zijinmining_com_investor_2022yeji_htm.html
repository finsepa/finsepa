 Operations LCE Resources Production Guidance
3Q Lithium Salar in Argentina 7.63 million tonnes (lithium ion concentration at 400mg/L cut-off) Phase 1 is designed to produce 20,000 tonnes of battery-grade lithium carbonate per annum, with its commissioning scheduled at the end of 2023. The project team has conducted preliminary studies on 40-60Ktpa capacity production. Process flows are to be optimized for higher lithium recovery and production.
Lakkor Tso Lithium Salar in Tibet, China 2.14 million tonnes (average lithium ion concentration at 270 mg/L) Phase 1 is designed to produce 20Ktpa of lithium carbonate, and the combined output will grow to 50Ktpa after Phase 2 is commissioned.
Xiangyuan Hard Rock Lithium Polymetallic Mine in Hunan, China 2.16 million tonnes The designed throughput is 10Mtpa. The mine is expected to produce 60-70Ktpa of LCE from lepidolite ores upon reaching nameplate capacity. The mine will first re-commission the existing 300Ktpa extraction and concentration system.
